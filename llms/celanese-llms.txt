## What an agent should know first

There is nothing here to call. As of 2026-09-06 Celanese exposes **no REST API, no GraphQL
endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no webhook or event surface, and no published
OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, WSDL or Protobuf contract**. Do not attempt to construct a base URL from
the corporate domain: `api.celanese.com` does not present a matching certificate, and
`developer.celanese.com` and `developers.celanese.com` do not resolve.

## Human-facing surfaces

- [Celanese corporate site](https://www.celanese.com): Company, products, applications and
  investor information. Sitecore-backed React application behind an Akamai edge.
- [Chemille digital materials assistant](https://materials.celanese.com): Natural-language
  material-selection assistant that helps engineers find a plastic or polymer by property,
  application and certification. A browser application for people — it publishes no API,
  no OpenAPI and no MCP endpoint.
